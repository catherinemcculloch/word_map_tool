Build Your Word Cloud

LLMs create word clouds, also known as vector embeddings, where words are tokens and vectors are the distances between tokens in space. Your brain is now the LLM. This tool allows you to create your own word cloud based on your lived experiences, ideas, and viewpoint. Drag words from the left column and place them in spatial relationship to one another — words closer together have more semantic similarity or contextual likeness, words further apart have less. There is no right location. Each word cloud will be unique to you.

Directions

LLMs create word clouds, also known as vector embeddings, where words are tokens and vectors are the distances between tokens in space. Your brain is now the LLM. This tool allows you the user to create your own word cloud based on your lived experiences, ideas, and viewpoint. Drag words from the column on the left and place them in spatial relationship to one another. Words closer together have more semantic similarity or contextual likeness. Words further apart on the screen have less. There is no right location. Remember this is based on your views, ideas, and lived experience. Each word cloud will be unique to each user. You can double click on each word to create a line between them. This line represents the sentence you are making.
